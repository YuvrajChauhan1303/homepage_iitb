Adaptive Game AI using PPO and the Impact of Hyperparameters in training of a PPO Model

AI / ML

Implemented reinforcement learning agents using (Proximal Policy Optimization) PPO in a Street Fighter-style game using Unity's ml-agents toolkit, and developed a street fighter game as a training and test bed. The work is currently under the process of being presented at a conference, initial drafts are ready and submitted. We have performed human-trials and made models fight other models to prove a model trained for more training steps might be better, given same hyperparameters, or models with lesser training steps, but better hyperparameter seclections can outperform a poor model trained with more steps.

Unity · PyTorch · Unity ML-Agents · PPO

On (k, n) Threshold based Pixel Coordinate Secret Image Sharing for Simple Images

Research

We Implemented a pixel-coordinate based multi-secret image sharing protocol capable of secure, lossless image reconstruction while maintaining constant-sized shares and histogram normalization. We identified a flaw with the previous scheme, proposed an optimization, and presented the same at the Int. Con. on Comp. & Comm. Net. (ICCCNT) 2025 @ IIT Indore. This work is soon to be published in the EPiC Series for Computing.
