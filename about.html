About Me

Hello, I'm Yuvraj. I've lived my entire life in Vadodara, Gujarat, and I've completed my primary education from Gujarat Public School, Atladara in Vadodara. During my school life, I picked up on a couple of hobbies, such as solving rubik's cubes (NERD ALERT!!!) and playing chess with my friends. I used to play basketball as well, and have played on the district level representing my school. Along the way I fell in love with Movies, specifically comic book movies or science fiction, or spy movies. So, if you have an extra ticket for the next Avenger's Movie, or when Batman 2 comes out. HMU!!! xD.

Coming to academics, I'm currently pursuing my Master of Technology in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at Indian Institute of Technology Bombay. Before landing here, I completed my Bachelor's in Computer Science and Engineering from IIIT Vadodara (International Campus Diu). Looking back, I think choosing Computer Science was inevitable. Too many spy movies xD.

These days my interests revolve around Cryptography, Security & Privacy (bcoz why not?), Machine Learning, and Computing Systems. During my undergraduate years, I got the opportunity to work on research projects involving continuous authentication, secret image sharing, and a few other interesting problems, which eventually led to conference publications and journal submissions. Research has been a surprisingly fun experience. Reading papers at 2 AM? Not so much.

Apart from research, the thing that I enjoy most is designing a high level overview of complex systems. I like thinking out loud, charting my ideas out, realising they're stupid and coming up with better designs. My experience in web development, and developing cryptographic systems using web development have been the most fun and frustrating projects. Pump me up with coffee, give me a white board, a marker, and a couple friends to discuss with, I'm basically House without his cane, wits and vicodin.

Outside academics, you'll probably find me listening to Indian hiphop, watching the 0.25x breakdown by Canadian Lad, or Over Analyzing One Piece. I'm always up for talking about technology, movies, basketball, or why Batman is clearly the best written comic book character. If you're into any of those, we'll probably get along.
